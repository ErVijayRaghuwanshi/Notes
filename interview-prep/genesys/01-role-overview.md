# Role Overview — Genesys Senior AI/ML Engineer

## Company Context

### Genesys at a Glance
- **Product**: Genesys Cloud — AI-powered Experience Orchestration platform
- **Domain**: Enterprise customer experience (CX) — contact centers, virtual agents, workforce engagement
- **Scale**: 7,500+ organizations, 100+ countries, 6,000+ employees globally
- **Core value proposition**: Empathetic, personalized customer experiences at scale

### Why This Role Matters
- Conversational AI is central to Genesys's product differentiation
- The team is transitioning toward **Agentic AI** — autonomous, tool-using agents
- Direct impact on enterprise customers' operational efficiency and customer satisfaction
- Opportunity to work at intersection of AI research and production systems

---

## Role Expectations (Decoded from JD)

### This is NOT just a research/experimentation role
The JD emphasizes:
- **Production systems** — "delivering production systems" mentioned explicitly
- **Software engineering depth** — system design, data structures, algorithms, architecture
- **Scale** — global, multi-tenant, enterprise-grade
- **Leadership** — owning technical initiatives, cross-functional collaboration

### What "Senior" means here
- 5+ years of software engineering with AI/ML systems expertise
- Ability to translate research into practical, scalable solutions
- Technical leadership without necessarily managing people
- Independent judgment on novel AI problems

## Skill Matrix

### Must-Have (Prepare Deeply)
- **Conversational AI/NLP** — intent classification, entity extraction, dialogue management, response generation
- **LLM application architecture** — prompting, retrieval-augmented generation, guardrails
- **System design** — distributed systems, scalability, reliability patterns
- **Software engineering fundamentals** — data structures, algorithms, clean code
- **MLOps** — deployment, monitoring, versioning, CI/CD for ML

### Good-to-Have (Prepare Credible Depth)
- **Multi-agent systems** — orchestration patterns, tool integration
- **Voice AI** — ASR/TTS, streaming, barge-in handling
- **AWS** — ECS/EKS, SageMaker, Lambda, Step Functions
- **Responsible AI** — bias detection, governance, compliance

### Stretch (Prepare Talking Points)
- Reinforcement learning for dialogue optimization
- Research publications or open-source contributions
- Patents in AI/ML
