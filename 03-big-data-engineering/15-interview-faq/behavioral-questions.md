# 🤝 Behavioral Questions for Big Data Interviews

These questions help you practice the decision-making and collaboration side of data-platform work.

---

**Q1: Tell me about a time you improved a slow or expensive pipeline.**  
Focus on measurement, root cause, trade-offs, and business impact.

**Q2: Describe a disagreement about batch vs streaming.**  
Show how you aligned the solution to user needs instead of tool preference.

**Q3: Tell me about a data-quality incident you helped resolve.**  
Explain detection, containment, root cause, and preventive improvements.

**Q4: Describe a migration from one data technology to another.**  
Talk about rollout safety, compatibility, and stakeholder communication.

**Q5: Tell me about a time you simplified an over-engineered data platform idea.**  
Show judgment, prioritization, and cost-awareness.

**Q6: Describe how you handled unclear ownership for a dataset or pipeline.**  
Good answers cover governance, accountability, and follow-through.

**Q7: Tell me about a time you had to backfill large volumes of historical data.**  
Highlight operational risk management, monitoring, and recovery planning.

**Q8: Describe a time you balanced analyst flexibility with platform governance.**  
This is a strong prompt for lakehouse, self-service SQL, and access control stories.

**Q9: Tell me about a platform decision you would change today.**  
Be honest, concrete, and focused on learning.

**Q10: How do you explain complex data-platform trade-offs to non-specialists?**  
Talk about diagrams, examples, risk framing, and plain-language communication.
