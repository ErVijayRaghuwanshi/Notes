## How To Practice

1. Spend 5 minutes on requirements and scope.
2. Spend 5 minutes on estimation.
3. Spend 10 minutes on high-level architecture.
4. Spend 10-15 minutes on deep dives and trade-offs.
5. End with bottlenecks, reliability, and future evolution.

## Expected Output For Each Case

- Problem statement and assumptions
- Functional and non-functional requirements
- Back-of-the-envelope estimates
- High-level design
- Data model and APIs
- Bottlenecks and optimizations
- Failure handling and trade-offs
- Interview answer summary
